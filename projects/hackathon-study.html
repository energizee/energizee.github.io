The brief

We were tasked with creating an application centered around Google's open-weight Gemma models within a 6 hour period. The main brief was to create a study companion that actually helped users study through actions and not just display information. The 1st place prize was $500, and I was up against teams of 2-3 people as a solo entrant, so I had to be very careful with my time and scope to ensure I could deliver a working product in the time frame.

What I built

The app I built was a personalised study companion called Momentum that was focused around actionable feedback rather than just displaing information. Gemma was the core here by holding a live updating personal profile on the user based on their data, and then providing actionable feedback such as study plans with specifcally generated questions that were automatically marked by Gemma.
