About me

How I started

I have been coding since I was around 8 years old. When I would play games on the PS3 with my dad and brother I would always have so many questions as to how it all worked. One day, probably to stop me asking so many questions, I got given a set of Python coding books and the rest is history!

What I've built

Grademap is my first proper large-scale project, and most of what I know about shipping something real I learned building it. Before that I was making games on Roblox, some solo and some collaborations, which is where I got used to writing code other people would actually use. Since then it's been a mix; a chat app and a text editor in Python, Java and C applications for university, and a handful of websites for coursework and for myself.

Outside of code

Outside of coding, I like to keep both my body and mind active. I enjoy the gym and running, and recently completed a half marathon! I am a huge animal lover, especially cats (as you can tell from this site). I also love music, and have been playing the bass guitar for years now, even performing live at my university.

What I'm looking for

Right now I'm looking for a graduate software engineering role or internship starting summer 2027, ideally somewhere I'd be writing production code early with people who'll review it properly. Happy to relocate.
